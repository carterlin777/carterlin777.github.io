Compact Vertical Spinner

Designed for impact stability through compact mass distribution

Design Strategy

This robot was designed around a compact mass distribution to reduce knockback during impacts. By minimizing the footprint and centralizing components, the robot maintains stability and quickly re-engages after collisions.

CAD Design

Master sketch constrained footprint and component placement. Parts were designed for tight packaging and structural integrity under impact.

Electronics

System diagram compared with real implementation. Tight packaging required careful wire routing to avoid interference with the weapon system.

Performance

In competition, the compact design reduced displacement during weapon impacts, allowing the robot to stay grounded and maintain aggression. This resulted in a controlled winning exchange.

Lessons Learned

Compact packaging improved stability but reduced accessibility during repairs. Future iterations would improve serviceability while maintaining a centralized mass distribution.
